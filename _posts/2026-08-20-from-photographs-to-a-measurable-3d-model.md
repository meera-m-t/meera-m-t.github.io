Reconstructing the three-dimensional shape and appearance of a real object from nothing but photographs: a classical pipeline recovers sparse metric structure, a learned multi-view depth model (CasDiffMVS) densifies it, and 3D Gaussian Splatting produces a photorealistic, free-viewpoint model. Every stage is validated against structured-light ground truth under the official DTU evaluation protocol, reaching sub-millimetre overall error.

The article is interactive &mdash; the 3D reconstructions and comparison videos are embedded live.
