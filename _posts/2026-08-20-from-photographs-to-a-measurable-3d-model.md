Take a handful of ordinary photographs of an object and rebuild the object itself: a classical reconstruction recovers a sparse 3D skeleton, a learned multi-view depth model densifies it, and 3D Gaussian Splatting turns it photorealistic — a model you can rotate, explore from any angle, and actually *measure*.
