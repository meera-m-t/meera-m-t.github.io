A conveyor line that knows exactly which physical unit sits under each camera &mdash; nothing printed, nothing applied, nothing to fall off. Each part is enrolled the first time a camera sees it; a metric appearance embedding and an open-set gate then re-identify it at every later station, even as added hardware progressively occludes the surface, while parts the line never enrolled are refused at the gate. On 502 real blocks imaged under two lighting conditions, retrieval reaches Rank-1 95.4% with mAP 0.968.

Live demos are embedded throughout the article.
