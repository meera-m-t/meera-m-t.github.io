What if a conveyor line could know exactly which physical unit sits under each camera — with no barcode, no QR sticker, nothing to peel off? Here, the part's own surface *is* the ID: enrolled the first time a camera sees it, recognized at every station after, even as more of that surface disappears under newly added hardware. An unenrolled stranger reaches the gate — and the gate refuses it.

Live demos included: watch identity survive all the way down the line, and retrieval run against 502 real parts.
